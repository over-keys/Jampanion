Derivation notice
-----------------

`FluidR3_Jampanion.sf3` was derived from the MIT-licensed FluidR3 SF3 bank
published by the Jacalz/fluid-soundfont project. The source bank identifies
itself as "Fluid R3 GM", credits Frank Wen, and contains the embedded comment
"Licensed under the MIT License."

Source bank SHA-256:
32039e039c2f708467a6f171fbfd9fecdcadfe40a2327837c391490c8de70021

The Jampanion subset retains only:

- Bank 0, program 0: Yamaha Grand Piano
- Bank 0, program 11: Vibraphone
- Bank 0, program 32: Acoustic Bass
- Bank 128, program 0: Standard drum kit

Unused presets, instruments and compressed Ogg Vorbis samples were removed
without transcoding the retained samples. The resulting bank contains 180
compressed samples.
